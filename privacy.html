Privacy Policy for TwinPane

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

🛡️ The TwinPane Privacy Guarantee

TwinPane was built upon strict Privacy by Design. We do not collect, harvest, store, transmit, or monetize any of your personal data, keystrokes, directories, or files. TwinPane functions 100% locally on your computer.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

TwinPane operates strictly on your Mac without remote connections. We do not collect:

  • No Personal Data: No user accounts, email addresses, names, or contact details.
  • No File Metadata or Contents: Your paths, directory names, filenames, file sizes, and file data never leave your local device.
  • No Telemetry or Usage Tracking: We do not monitor how often you open the app, which hotkeys you press, or what file operations you run.
  • No Identifiers or IP Addresses: We do not log IP addresses, device identifiers, serial numbers, or advertising IDs.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: There are zero analytics SDKs or monitoring tools integrated into the app.

2. Local File Management & Security

When you browse, copy, move, rename, delete, or inspect files with TwinPane:

  • All operations are executed strictly using native Rust and operating system APIs on your machine.
  • TwinPane operates strictly within the macOS App Sandbox, ensuring it only interacts with directories and files you explicitly authorize or access.
  • No network requests are generated by any file operations.

3. Third-Party Services & Network Access

TwinPane contains zero third-party trackers, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry frameworks. The application binary contains no background networking servers or reporting daemons.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because TwinPane does not collect or store any personal data whatsoever, it is fully safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any revisions to this Privacy Policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Our commitment to 100% offline data integrity and zero tracking will never change.
