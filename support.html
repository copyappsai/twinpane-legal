Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

⌨️ What are the standard keyboard shortcuts in TwinPane?

TwinPane adheres to the classic dual-pane standard: Tab switches active panes; F3 views file preview; F4 opens in text editor; F5 copies to destination pane; F6 moves to destination pane; F7 creates a new folder; F8 deletes; and Cmd + T opens a new tab.

🔒 How does macOS permissions / App Sandbox work?

TwinPane runs securely inside Apple's App Sandbox. When accessing custom external drives or special system folders for the first time, macOS will present a standard system confirmation dialog to grant read/write access.

🌐 How do I change the interface language?

You can switch languages at any time by clicking the globe icon (🌐) on the top toolbar or opening Settings. TwinPane includes 12 native translations.

🛡️ Does TwinPane collect telemetry or send data over the network?

Absolutely not. TwinPane is completely offline. It contains no tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or cloud dependencies. Your files and paths never leave your local Mac.

💻 What are the minimum system requirements?

TwinPane runs on macOS 11.0 (Big Sur) or newer, natively compiled for both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs.
